# Wikipedia - Organization System Analysis

---
## Primary Resources Being Organized

Wikipedia is a free public online encyclopedia that organizes its information using individual articles about various specific topics. 

The primary resources being organized include **articles regarding people, places, scientific concepts, technology, historical events, entertainment, and much more.** These resources are displayed as a singular article, but there are connections to other articles within the primary text to display additional information related to the main topic. Other resources being organized are pages that involve user discussions, user pages, and site administration pages. 

One of the primary resources Wikipedia organizes and provides users with are **primary sources and external references.** Since Wikipedia is a secondary source, it is a great tool to find primary sources and references on a specific subject. 

---
## Primary Interactions Supported

The primary interactions Wikipedia supports include **fact-checking, browsing, and research.** 

Fact-checking is the primary interaction supported by Wikipedia, because of the extensive references and citations throughout the article and at the bottom in the "References" section. Any user can quickly find the sources behind the information in the article and check the credibility of it for themselves. 

Browsing is also one of the primary interactions supported by Wikipedia. Their main page has sections that display articles relating to current news, featured articles, events from today's date, and a "Did you know..." section. This all promotes the user to keep interacting with the site and browse new topics and information. 

Wikipedia supports doing research as well, since the user can find a specific topic and then look further into the details, such as related events or important people and locations. Within any article of a topic, there are several subsections providing information that help make the researching process more efficient. 

---
## Classification System 

Wikipedia utilizes **both a hierarchical and a faceted classification system.** 

A hierarchical system is displayed when pages are organized into broad categories and subcategories. This helps the user find relating topics on a broad scale or a more refined search into a smaller subtopic. 

The user also can search through information using a faceted classification system. Wikipedia offers many advanced search filter options that can refine a search to genres, years, locations, etc. 

This system is effective for Wikipedia's massive and diverse audience because it supports both specific targeted searching and information discovery while browsing. It could potentially be more effective, but it would be a very difficult task with such an enormous content library and diverse audience. 

---
## Relationship Between Resources

The main way resource relationships are represented on Wikipedia are through **hyperlinks** within the article. This creates a massive network of related information that supports a relationship between content all throughout the system. 

**"See also" sections and categories** are also present, to give users another pathway to find related pages and topics. 

---
## Analysis Conclusion

Overall, Wikipedia utilizes multiple classification systems, supports numerous interactions, and represents various relationships between resources. This approach is necessary due to the massive amount of resources Wikipedia has to organize and constantly update on a regular basis.
