# Product Performance

Product performance measures how video games perform after being released and how those results affect their overall success in the consumer market. There are several ways to measure a game's performance, such as sales, critical reception, player retention, and the strength of its launch. 

These factors are all individually important because a game can succeed in one area while simultaneously struggling in another. For example, critics often give excellent reviews to a game, yet that game will still end up selling poorly or perform under expectations. In contrast, many video games become largely successful and are praised by the general public, while having mixed reviews from critics. Analyzing these different factors of a products performance helps to provide a more clear picture what makes a game a success or a failure commercially.
