# Critical Reception

Critical reception in the video game industry is the response received from professional reviewers, journalists, and other critics. These reviews are important because it influences the way the consumer perceives the game and can be the deciding factor to whether or not they purchase the product. While a good or bad review does not always entail how the product will perform, critic reception is a major contributing factor to a video game's reputation and has significant impact on how the game performs in the market.

#### Consumer Decision-Making

Consumers use reviews as one of the deciding factors behind their decision-making process when deciding to purchase a game or not. Critics dissect the product and recommend whether or not the user should buy the game. Consumers also look at the reviews from other players, making user reviews sometimes even more influential than those of critics. 

## Positive and Negative Reception

Positive critical reception brings more attention to the game, encouraging consumers to become interested and more likely to find out more about the product. In contrast, negative reception can push away consumers who were perhaps already on the edge about buying the game. Both positive and negative reception has direct impact on [[sales-performance|Sales Performance]], as consumers use these reviews when making their purchasing decisions. 

Even though critical reception is important, it does not always directly align with the commercial success of a product. Games with mixed reviews have found large audiences and commercial success, while other games with higher reception end up with a smaller number of consumers. This means that critical reception should be taken into consideration along with other factors, such as [[player-retention|Player Retention]], marketing, sales, and other measures of [[commercial-success-and-failure|Commercial Success and Failure]].
