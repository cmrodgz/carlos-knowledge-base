# Overview

Video games can build communities for players to communicate, interact, and share experiences with one another. Consumer engagement is how the player continues to interact with the game or the community after they have purchased and played the game. A game with a strong community and player engagement can help a game be successful long-term and increase it's reputation in the market. 

## Online Communities and Social Media

A community created around a specific game can help expose the game to new players and encourage current players to keep engaging with the product. These communities also allow development companies to communicate more effectively with their audience and receive extensive feedback about the game. 

Social media helps to expand the game's community from beyond the game itself. The game becomes more visible to new and existing audiences when users share clips, guides, screenshots, and other content online. This connects consumer communities and engagement to [[media-marketing|Media Marketing]]. 

## Consumer Engagement and Retention

The community surrounding a game can be the driving force for a game's continued activity and engagement. This can give players reasons apart from the game itself to keep coming back tot he game and engaging with the content. These communities are especially important for games following business models such as live-service or multiplayer games. The communities made by consumers directly influence a game's success with [[player-retention|Player Retention]]. 

## Consumer Communities and Commercial Performance

A game with a strong community helps keep a game visible months and years after it's initial release. This drives consumers to recommend the game to others and interact with the content being posted in the community. 

Although a strong community is an important factor to having a commercially successful game, it still relies on various other factors including competition, game quality, pricing, business models, and marketing in order to be a success. These consumer communities directly influence the [[sales-performance|Sales Performance]] and overall [[commercial-success-and-failure|Commercial Success and Failure]] of a video game.
