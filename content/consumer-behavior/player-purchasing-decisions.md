# Overview

A player considers a multitude of factors when deciding to purchase a certain video game. A games genre, price, reviews, advertising, reputation, platform, and recommendations all contribute to a player's decision-making process. 

Understanding a players decision-making process is important because we can identify what causes some games to attract a large player base while others struggle. 

## Price and Value

Price is one of the main factors for players when it comes to purchasing a game. Money is a limited resource, so a player has to weigh if the price of the game provides them with enough value to warrant the purchase. A game is valued by the amount of content, quality, and entertainment that will be received by the consumer. 

Different business models also change the way players value games. If a game is free-to-play, it usually makes up for the cost by requiring players to spend more time playing the game and spending money on microtransactions. These differences in models connect player decision-making to [[premium-priced-games|Premium Priced Games]] and [[free-to-play-and-microtransactions|Free-to-Play and Microtransactions]].

## Game Reviews and Reputation

Another major factor for a player's purchasing decision-making process is the reviews surrounding the games quality and gameplay. Games with positive reviews promote players to purchase the game, while negative reviews can end up pushing potential buyers away. That is why [[audience-reviews-and-word-of-mouth|Audience Reviews and Word of Mouth]] and [[critical-reception|Critical Reception]] are major factors when a player is considering purchasing a game. 

## Marketing

If a player does not know a game exists or when it is being released, they can not purchase the game. This makes marketing an essential part for a video game to be successful, as the game can be introduced to potential players and generate more engagement before the game is even released. This makes [[video-game-advertising|Video Game Advertising]] and [[pre-launch-marketing|Pre-Launch Marketing]] big factors when it comes to a player's purchasing decisions. 

## Player Purchasing Decisions and Commercial Performance

A player considers all of these factors when deciding whether or not a game is worth the purchase. It is important for companies to understand how to balance aspects such as pricing and how much money they put into marketing to have a chance at being commercially successful. Having effective control of a player's purchasing decisions directly affects the games initial sales and the long-term commercial performance it will have.
