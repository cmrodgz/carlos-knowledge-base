# Overview

Audience reviews, rating, and word of mouth all influence the way players perceive video games and their behavior towards the product. Players look at user ratings, videos, professional reviews, and recommendations from online communities to evaluate whether or not a game is worth its price. 

## Professional Reviews

A consumer commonly looks to professional game reviewers to decide whether or not to purchase a game. These reviews are especially important for a game because it contributes to the game's overall public reputation. Positive professional reviews increase consumer engagement, while negative reviews make potential consumers behave more cautiously. This is why a game's [[critical-reception|Critical Reception]] is so influential to how the consumer engages with the product. 

## User Ratings

User ratings let other potential consumers see how players are responding toward a game. These are useful to consumers because it can provide a quick way to see the general feelings the majority of the audience has towards a game, although they can often lack in-depth reasoning or explanations as to why they feel that way. 

These ratings from players are often taken into account even more than those of professional reviewers, making user ratings one of the most important factors behind how consumers view a game. 

## Word of Mouth

Word of mouth is when people share their experiences and opinions with others. Word of mouth is one of the most powerful forces in the entertainment industry, as positive word of mouth can almost be a substitute for strong marketing campaigns. 

By having a consumer share positive experiences and opinions with others, they are basically marketing the game to a new audience and convincing them to try the game as well. This is especially effective when trusted creators or personal friends are sharing these positive opinions, as consumers value opinions from people they know or follow. This provides a direct correlation between word of mouth, [[player-purchasing-decisions|Player Purchasing Decisions]] and [[media-marketing|Media Marketing]]. 

## Audience Reviews and Commercial Performance

These reviews and word of mouth from audience can significantly boost a game's reputation and sales. These can also increase how often the game is recommended to other players and if the game will continue being discussed after release.
