# Consumer Behavior

This section analyzes how consumers make decisions involving video games and the way in which they interact with companies, games, and other players. It is important to understand consumer behavior because a game being commercially successful requires consumers to purchase, play, recommend, and have continuous engagement with the product. 

This section is directly related to [[market-saturation-and-competition|Market Saturation and Competition]] because consumers can change their behaviors due to the various options competing for their engagement and spending. [[promotion-and-marketing/index|Promotion and Marketing]] is also connected to consumer behavior because promotional campaigns can influence a consumers engagement levels and purchasing decision-making.
