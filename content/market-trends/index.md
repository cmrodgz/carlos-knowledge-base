# Market Trends

The Market Trends section examines the changes and conditions which influence the video game industry. This section will explore the context behind how new business practices and technologies affect the overall industry, why the market continues to grow, and why an increase in competition can make achieving commercial success more challenging for games. 

Factors that influence the video game market include: consumer preferences, technological advancement, competition, business models, and distribution methods. This section will provide the foundation for the entire knowledge base regarding the environment in which video games are produced and sold.
