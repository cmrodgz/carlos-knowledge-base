# Overview

The video game industry has grown from a small entertainment market into one of the major entertainment industries in the world. Many factors have contributed to this exponential growth, including technological advancements, better access to the internet, changes in consumer behavior, and the increase in mobile devices. The video game industry's growth has greatly benefited from becoming accessible on various platforms, such as personal computers, phones, consoles, and even on cloud-based services. 

Due to the growth of this industry, there has also been greater financial risks involved in the production and publishing process of games. There is now more competition than ever for the consumers attention and money, but there is also more opportunities with a continuously growing audience. Having an understanding of how the industry has and will continue to grow is vital to comprehend why some games are commercially successful while many others fail. 

## Stages of Industry Growth

The video game market has seen many important points of expansion, resulting in the growth we have seen in the modern day. In the earlier stages, video games were primarily connected to arcades and home consoles. As technology advanced, gaming at home became more accessible and more games were being produced. 

Another important point for video game growth came when personal computers became more accessible for consumers. This opened a new market for gaming and would later result in games being distributed digitally without the need for physical media. 

The audience grew once again once smartphones were introduced and became another new major market for video games. Gamers no longer needed a gaming PC or a dedicated gaming system to play games, which stamped mobile gaming as a major part of the overall gaming industry moving forward. 

The modern day industry is still finding ways to grow their audience. Subscription programs, digital storefronts, and cloud services are some of the new ways that games are being distributed and consumed in the market. 

## Video Game Accessibility

The modern video game industry has evolved to become accessible in every platform, allowing for the maximum amount of exposure to new potential consumers. These platforms include:

- **Console Gaming** - Still a major force in the market, as companies compete with exclusive titles and advanced hardware for consumer engagement.
- **PC Gaming** - A powerful market that houses a large and diverse audience, giving developers more opportunities for success. 
- **Mobile Gaming** - A large but distinct market from the rest, as the audience does not need specialized gaming hardware to engage in the video game market. 
- **Cloud-Based Gaming** - Allows a different way to access games without needing extra hardware or software, making gaming more accessible.
