# Overview

The video game market constantly sees new trends and changes with the introduction of new technology, distribution methods, consumer preferences, and business strategies. While these new trends create new opportunities for developers, there is also more risks to be accounted for as well. It is important for companies to weigh the risk and reward of emerging trends in the video game industry, as it could affect marketing strategies, consumer demand, development choices, and long-term revenue. 

In the modern video game market, there are several trends which have become highly influential and important to the industry as a whole. These include live-service games, mobile gaming, subscription services, digital distribution, and cross-platform play. The foundation for these emerging trends result from the [[growth-of-the-video-game-industry|Growth of the Video Game Industry]].

## Live-Service

Live-service games function by receiving ongoing updates of content after they have been released. These games create an opportunity for continuous revenue and consumer engagement, but they require continued investment throughout the games life cycle. Being able to keep players engaged with the product long-term is directly connected to a product's performance and its [[player-retention|Player Retention]].

## Subscription Services

This emerging trend has also become a major part of the modern video game market. Consumers are now able to pay a recurring fee to access a library of games instead of purchasing each game individually. This trend is one of the more complicated, because while it can produce recurring revenue for a company, it may also lead to the consumer being less willing to purchase games at full price on launch. This connection involving certain market trends is important to consider when deciding on which [[business-models/index|Business Models]] may have a greater chance of succeeding. 

## Cross-Platform Play

This trend allows players on different gaming platforms to come together and play their favorite games. The benefit of this trend is increasing the size of the community of a game and therefore make the game more appealing for consumers. The potential downside for this however is having to go through more testing and development time to make sure every platform works with the game. Balancing these decisions when making a game is tied directly to [[development-decisions|Development Decisions]] and [[development-costs|Development Costs]].

## Mobile Gaming 

This is one of the major trends to have majorly impacted the overall video game market. Being able to play games on smartphones allows a new audience to engage in the industry without need dedicated gaming hardware. Many of the most successful mobile games use a [[free-to-play-and-microtransactions|Free-to-Play and Microtransactions]] approach to generate continuous revenue and reach more consumers. 

## Digital Distribution

This trend has become a standard aspect of the video game market. Being able to purchase and download games completely online has expanded the market even more and allowed developers to distribute their games independently. 

## Emerging Trends and Commercial Performance

All of these trends influence the way the modern video game market operates and grows each day. These new development in how companies reach consumers and generate revenue impact how a game ends up performing in the consumer market. Having an in-depth understanding of these trends helps us understand why certain games succeed or fail commercially.
