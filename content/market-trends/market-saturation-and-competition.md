# Overview

There are a large amount of publishers, developers, and platforms all fighting for the same consumer's engagement and spending. As the industry has grown, the audience for games is larger, but it is also harder for individual games to stand out. 

Market competition is the rivalry between companies and platforms to be more attractive to the consumer. When a market is saturated, it means that a particular market has numerous competing options for the consumer to choose from. 

## Consumer Spending

Companies focus a lot of effort on convincing consumers that their product provides enough value to warrant a purchase and their money. These companies understand that the average consumer has a limited amount of money for entertainment and that when they spend on a certain game, there will be less money left for purchases on another game. These decisions are examined more closely in [[player-purchasing-decisions|Player Purchasing Decisions]]. 

## Consumer Engagement

Consumers do not only just have limited money, but they also only have a limited amount of free time to engage with the product. Spending a certain amount of time in a game means less time available in another game. So, companies must find ways to keep players engaged and attract new ones simultaneously. This demonstrates a direct connection between market competition, [[player-retention|Player Retention]], and [[consumer-communities-and-engagement|Consumer Communities and Engagement]].

## Market Saturation

A market becomes saturated when there are many options for a consumer to choose from. This means that even well-made games can sometimes underperform because of the number of options in the market. Companies must find ways to get consumers to notice their game in saturated markets and pick their product over the rest. 

## Market Conditions and Commercial Performance

Market saturation and competition do not entirely determine whether or not a game succeeds or fails. However, it does shape the environment for the game in which it has to compete with others for a consumer's limited money and engagement. The [[product-performance/index|Product Performance]] is measured in several ways to determine how a game withstood market conditions and the resulting commercial outcome.
