# Overview

Pre-launch marketing is the promotional campaign that takes place before the launch of a game to create awareness, encourage potential consumers to consider buying the game, and build up additional hype. 

This is important in a competitive market because it can give a game an early advantage over their competitors before the game even reaches the market. 

## Raising Awareness

Video game companies announce games months and even years before their scheduled release and gradually continue to release bits of content while the game goes through the development process. The purpose of this marketing approach is to create long-term discussions around the game all the way until its eventual release. This makes pre-launch marketing one of the most important parts of a games overall [[video-game-advertising|Video Game Advertising]] plan. 

## Building Hype

To build hype means to create a high level of anticipation and excitement surrounding the release of an upcoming product. Hype can be built through epic cinematic trailers, gameplay demonstrations, social media campaigns, and major announcements surrounding the game. 

Influencers are in important tool for creating additional hype for a game, as when a creator has positive remarks and reviews over an upcoming release, their audience can become more excited to try it for themselves. However, when a influencer has negative reviews about an upcoming game, it can result in less hype instead. This makes [[media-marketing|Media Marketing]] an important aspect of both building hype and the overall pre-launch marketing campaign. 

## Consumer Expectations

While pre-launch marketing brings more attention and interest to a game, it can also end up raising the expectations of the consumer. This is a good thing when the release of the game ends up meeting or exceeding these expectations. However, when the final product is released and it does not meet these expectations, the results can be detrimental to a games chance of being commercially successful. This makes pre-launch marketing a major factor in a game's [[critical-reception|Critical Reception]] and [[audience-reviews-and-word-of-mouth|Audience Reviews and Word of Mouth]]. 

## Pre-Launch Marketing and Commercial Performance

While strong pre-launch marketing can help raise awareness and attract consumers, it does not guarantee that a game will experience high sales numbers or long-term success. Many additional factors are taking place that impact how the game will perform such as consumer demand, pricing, the quality of the final product, and competition. The connection between all these factors and pre-launch marketing influence the [[sales-performance|Sales Performance]] and [[launch-performance|Launch Performance]] a game has.
