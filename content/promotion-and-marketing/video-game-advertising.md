# Overview

An important part of promoting a video game and introducing it to potential consumers is advertising. This is used by companies to identify the target audience for a game, encourage consumers to purchase it, and to showcase what the game offers. 

Advertising is especially important when there is a lot of [[market-saturation-and-competition|Market Saturation and Competition]], as good advertising can win over consumer engagement from rival companies. 

## Targeted Advertising

Effective advertising focuses on the audience that is most likely to engage with a particular game. Factors such as age, genres, gaming platforms, and previous interest in the franchise are all considered when advertising to a targeted audience. By targeting advertisement to consumers who already have interest in the product, the promotional budget the company has will be spend more effectively and there will be a higher chance of successfully convincing a consumer to buy a product. 

## Advertising and Expectations

An advertising campaign can result in influencing what a consumer expects from a game before purchasing it. These expectation change how consumers evaluate the final product on release, which could be beneficial or harmful depending on the quality of the product at launch. This creates a direct connection between [[critical-reception|Critical Reception]] and [[audience-reviews-and-word-of-mouth|Audience Reviews and Word of Mouth]]. 

## Advertising and Commercial Performance

A company must decide how much of their budget will be allocated toward advertising their game. Having a large advertisement budget means that more consumers will see the game, but it will also increase expenses and that means it will need to make more revenue to become a commercial success. Their advertising expenses directly influence [[development-costs|Development Costs]] and the game's probability of [[commercial-success-and-failure|Commercial Success and Failure]].
