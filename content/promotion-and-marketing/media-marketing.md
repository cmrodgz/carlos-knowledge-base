# Overview

One of the major tools for promoting video games in the modern video game market are influencers and social media. Using social media platforms, users can create and share videos that help bring more potential players to the game while simultaneously increasing the conversations about the game online. 

This is different from traditional advertising because there can direct interaction between the company, creators, and consumers. 

## Social Media

A game company can use social media to share visuals and announcements about a game to keep consumers interested and engaged with the game before and after release. The company can also use social media to directly answer a user's question and read the feedback provided by the audience. This sets a direct connection between media marketing and [[consumer-communities-and-engagement|Consumer Communities and Engagement]]. 

## Influencers and Content Creators

Content creators and influencers have existing audiences who may not be introduced to a certain game. By using them to promote the game, the game is introduced to a brand new audience. The influencer can also show viewers the way the game works, and end up helping them decide whether or not purchasing the game is worth it. This makes it one of the best ways to influence [[player-purchasing-decisions|Player Purchasing Decisions]]. 

## Word of Mouth

Social media is one of the best ways to spread positive word of mouth about a game. If people are raving about a new great game, they will want to share their positive experience with others and influence them to purchase the game as well. This makes social media very connected to [[audience-reviews-and-word-of-mouth|Audience Reviews and Word of Mouth]]. 


## Media Marketing and Commercial Performance

Marketing campaigns on social media can be great for gaining more visibility and being introduced into a brand new established audience. However, there is a lot of room for negative discussions to surround the game instead, hurting the games image and reputation. When word of mouth is bad, the game is at risk of underperforming in the market and has a greater chance to fail commercially. This makes media marketing a big factor in a games [[critical-reception|Critical Reception]].
