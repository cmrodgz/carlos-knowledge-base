# Overview

A game's development timeline can range from months to several years depending on the goal and complexity of a project. A games development timeline is directly related to [[development-costs|Development Costs]], because the more delays and longer development timeline results in higher costs in the development process. 

## Causes of Development Delays

- Staffing problems
- Technical difficulties
- Changes in project scope
- New technology requirements
- Additional changes requested during development
- Additional testing
- Difficulty coordinating development team

## Financial Impact

Delays to a game can majorly increase the expenses for a project. Companies must balance the potential benefit of having more development time with the downside of the financial expense of a longer production timeline. This creates a direct connection between development timelines and [[development-decisions|Development Decisions]]. 

## Delays and Marketing

A game going through delays involving the release date can result in many promotional campaigns and marketing plans being rescheduled and altered. These delays can hurt the excitement and hype for a project, making it a major factor when dealing with [[pre-launch-marketing|Pre-Launch Marketing]]. 

## Delays and Launch Peformance

When a game is delayed, there is a good chance that it will affect how consumers view and respond to the project. Significant delays can result in players losing interest or lowering their expectations for the final product. However, delays can end up being beneficial for the developers as they can end up delivering a more refined and polished final product at launch. Careful planning of release dates and additional preparation can help the games [[launch-performance|Launch Performance]] and [[critical-reception|Critical Reception]].
