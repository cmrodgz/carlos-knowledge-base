# Overview

Game passes and subscription services provide players with access to games or game features for a recurring payment. The player no longer has to purchase every game individually, but instead receive access to a large library pf games as long as they keep their subscription active. 

This business model has become another way for video game companies to distribute games to players, create consistent revenue, and give players another pathway to access games.

## How It Works

These subscription services require the consumer to pay a monthly or yearly fee. After they pay this fee, they gain access to a large library of games without having to purchase each one separately, therefore, potentially saving them money. This is an important [[player-purchasing-decisions|Player Purchasing Decision]] because the player must decide if the value of the subscription is worth the cost. 

## Impact on Industry

These subscription services for games allow game developers another way to make money from their games. A development company gets paid to be included in a subscription service, which can introduce the game and studio to a new larger audience. This is another way for a game to be advertised to new potential consumers and grow the audience for the game. 

## Game Passes and Commercial Performance

Subscription services and game passes offer players a alternate way to engage with games by paying a recurring fee. This can drive consumers to engage with more games they would not have before, helping to keep players playing games and create opportunities for generating more revenue. This business model helps increase [[player-retention|Player Retention]] and [[consumer-communities-and-engagement|Consumer Communities and Engagement]] in the video game market by offering variety to how players can access games.
