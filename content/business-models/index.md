# Business Models

This section analyzes the different models video game companies use to generate revenue from their products. The business models a game has influences it's development decisions, marketing strategy, pricing, and relationship with consumers. 

Business models relate closely to [[player-purchasing-decisions|Player Purchasing Decisions]] because different pricing systems influence how a player decides to spend their money. They also connect to [[emerging-trends-in-the-video-game-market|Emerging Trends in the Video Game Market]], where advancements in technology and increased consumer expectations create new business models for game monetization and distribution.

These three pages are different approaches video game companies take to generate revenue from their games.
